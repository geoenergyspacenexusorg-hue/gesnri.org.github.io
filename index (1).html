Geo-energy and space science research for inclusive, sustainable development.

GeoEnergy Space Nexus Research Institute is a registered, independent, non-profit and non-political research institute based in the Gambella Region of Ethiopia. We work where geothermal and geo-energy systems, space science and geospatial technology meet.

Partner with us Meet the team

Our vision

A scientifically self-reliant Gambela and all of Ethiopia that harnesses the convergence of geo-energy resources and space science to power inclusive, sustainable national development.

Our mission

To conduct, promote and disseminate rigorous, policy-relevant research at the intersection of geothermal and geo-energy systems, satellite and space science, and geospatial technology, and to build the human and institutional capacity of Gambela, Ethiopia in these fields.

Projects and research areas

Five areas guide our research, training and field work.

Space science

Space science and satellite applications research, and building local capability in the field.

Geospatial technology

Remote sensing and geospatial data analysis for energy, agriculture, disaster risk management and development planning.

Geo-energy

Research on geothermal energy, geo-resources and other energy systems relevant to Ethiopia and the Horn of Africa.

Climate resilience

Earth and space science evidence to inform environmental and energy policy and help communities prepare for climate and disaster risks.

Humanitarian impact

Support for humanitarian work with communities in need in Ethiopia, guided by humanity, neutrality, impartiality and independence.

Objectives set out in our bylaws

  1. Research on geothermal energy, geo-resources and other energy systems relevant to Ethiopia and the Horn of Africa.
  2. Research in space science, satellite applications, remote sensing and geospatial data analysis for energy, agriculture, disaster risk management and development planning.
  3. Interdisciplinary “nexus” research linking earth and space sciences to inform energy, environmental and technology policy.
  4. Building the capacity of Ethiopian researchers, graduate students and technicians through training, fellowships, joint supervision and exchange programmes.
  5. Independent, evidence-based technical advice to government, the private sector and development partners, on request.
  6. Laboratories, field research stations, data centres and observation facilities, as resources permit.
  7. Publishing findings in peer-reviewed journals, policy briefs and public engagement.
  8. Partnerships with universities, research institutions and development partners in Ethiopia and abroad.
  9. Mobilising and managing resources in accordance with Ethiopian law.
  10. Humanitarian support for communities in need in Ethiopia.
